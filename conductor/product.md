# Initial Concept

Autonomous AI Research Workspace with NotebookLM (NLM) Grounding and Sequential Thinking MCP Suite.

## Product Definition
The system establishes a rigorous, production-grade AI research workspace governed by Master Orchestration and JudgeGuard. It integrates NotebookLM as the authoritative single source of truth for research knowledge bases, alongside sequential-thinking reasoning and full MCP server toolchains.

## Target Audience
Autonomous agents, researchers, and engineers performing deep empirical research, literature review, and complex multi-step reasoning.

## Core Capabilities
1. **NotebookLM (NLM) Deep Grounding:** Direct access to Google NotebookLM notebooks for citations, summaries, audio overviews, and factual verification.
2. **Sequential Thinking MCP:** Multi-step cognitive chain-of-thought with dynamic revision and structured hypothesis testing.
3. **JudgeGuard Safety & Orchestration:** Multi-layer gatekeeper enforcing user sovereignty, audit trails, and atomic task chunking.
4. **Autonomous Bootstrap:** Self-verifying environment startup checking tools, credentials, and connectivity.
